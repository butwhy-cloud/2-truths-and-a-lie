2 Truths & a Lie

Step-by-step guide

You get ready once, in about 20 minutes. After that, playing is a few clicks.

📄 Download this guide as a PDF🌍 Hierdie gids in Afrikaans

🧰 What you need

  • A computer or laptop with Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. It runs the game, so not a phone or tablet.
  • Internet on the computer and on the phones.
  • A phone for every player. No app needed.
  • A projector or big screen is nice, but not needed: every phone shows everything.

⚠️ Before you start: the network

Phones join most easily on the same Wi-Fi as the computer. Always test with 2 or 3 phones first. Phones on mobile data, and some school and office Wi-Fi, need a relay: see Extras at the end.

Part 1Get ready

1

Download the files

On the computer, click the game in your language, and the spreadsheet. They go to your Downloads folder.
🎮 The game (English)🎮 The game (Afrikaans)📊 The spreadsheet

💡 In this PDF, each button downloads its file straight away. Do it on the computer you'll play on, not on your phone.

⬇ Your download has started: look in your Downloads folder. Nothing happened? Click the flashing button.

💡 Does the spreadsheet open in a viewer instead? Click Download in that window.

2

Make a folder

Make a new folder, for example on your Desktop: right-click → New → Folder. Move the game and the spreadsheet into it.

💡 It must be a new folder: the game can't open the Desktop, Documents or Downloads themselves. Keep only one spreadsheet in it.

3

Fill in the spreadsheet

Open the spreadsheet. Each person gets one row: their name and three things about them. Two are true and one is a lie.

Don't changeFill in
  • Don't change the top row or the numbers in the Person column.
  • Don't write down which one is the lie. Each person tells everyone during the game.
  • Delete the rows you don't use, then save.

💡 Ask everyone for their three statements before the day.

4

Add the photos

Put a photo of each person in the folder. Rename it to match the Person column: person 1, person 2 and so on. Only type the name: the ending (.jpg) stays by itself.

Part 2Play

5

Open the game

Double-click the game file. If it opens in another browser, right-click the file → Open with → Google Chrome (or Edge).
Click 📁 Choose the folder, pick your folder and allow it. It may say "upload", but nothing leaves your computer. The numbers at the bottom are your people.

💡 A number with an orange ring has no photo: check the photo's name.

6

Let everyone join

Click 📱 Live, then Switch live on. When Ready: players can join shows in green, players scan the QR code with their phone camera and type their name. When everyone's in, click Start ▶.

💡 No big screen? Click Copy link and send it to the players, for example on WhatsApp.

7

Play a round

Click a number at the bottom, or 🎲 Random. Everyone has 30 seconds to tap the lie on their phone.
Then the person says which one was the lie. Click that number (1, 2 or 3) on the card: after 30 seconds the numbers pulse. Every phone shows the points: a right answer gets 70 to 100, the faster the more.
  • The person on the card knows the lie, so they don't vote.
  • Clicked the wrong number? Click the right one.
  • Click 🏆 any time for the scores.
8

Finish

Click 🏁 End, then OK. Everyone sees the final scores. Save the scores when the Save window opens (or click 📥 Save results (Excel)). Click 🌐 Save results page for a page with the podium and fun facts, to share. Then close the game: nothing is kept apart from what you saved.

Extras (only if you want them)

🎨

Your logo

Put your school's or company's logo in the folder and name it logo. It shows on the card. On your own card you drag it into place.
🔁

A relay (for mobile data)

Got a relay link? Do this once on the computer you'll play on:
• Playing online? Open the link.
• Playing with the downloaded file? Click 📱 Live, then 🔁 Relay link…, paste the link and click OK.
The join screen then says 🔁 Relay: on. Keep the link private. No link? Setting up a relay takes some tech know-how: Playing across different networks.

🛟 If something goes wrong

Phones can't join
Check they're on the same Wi-Fi as the computer. Still not working? That Wi-Fi probably blocks devices from reaching each other, which is common on guest, school and office Wi-Fi. Use another Wi-Fi, a phone hotspot that everyone joins, or a relay (see Extras).
A photo doesn't show
Check its name matches the Person column (person 1, person 2…) and that it's JPG or PNG.
"Can't open this folder because it contains system files"
Make a new folder inside it and choose that one.
The game closed by accident
Open it again within 15 minutes and choose the folder. The game carries on.
The Excel isn't in my folder
Look in Downloads.

🔒 Your spreadsheet, photos and results stay on your computer. Nothing is uploaded. During a round, the computer sends that person's statements, name and photo straight to the phones, and that's all.
